Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we store your Google display name and a unique user ID. We also store your game progress (kebab count, upgrades, city progress) in Firebase Firestore so it syncs across devices. We do not collect your email address, phone number, or any payment information.

Chat messages

Messages you send in the global or city chat are stored in Firestore and visible to all players. Messages are automatically deleted after 10 minutes. Do not share personal information in chat.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the game experience — syncing progress, showing your name on the leaderboard, and enabling multiplayer chat. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

This game uses Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, and hosting. Firebase's privacy policy applies: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Data deletion

You can delete your account data at any time using the "Reset Game" button in the Settings tab, which removes your Firestore document. You can also sign out to stop syncing.

Age requirement

This game is intended for players aged 13 and older. If you are under 13, please do not sign in or use the chat features.

Terms of use

By playing Kebab Clicker Empire you agree not to exploit bugs, use automated scripts, or attempt to manipulate the leaderboard. We reserve the right to reset or ban accounts that violate fair play. The game is provided as-is with no warranty.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the project repository or contact the developer directly.
